Meet Sarah & Olivier

It began as a whispered dream between the banyan trees. Sarah, an artist with a soul for Mauritian heritage, and Olivier, a former chef seeking the quietude of the lagoon, envisioned more than just a boutique hotel.

They sought to build a sanctuary where time is measured by the tide rather than the clock. Every corner of Cazhibiscus reflects their personal touch—from the hand-picked artisanal pottery to the secret garden paths designed for morning reflection.

S. & O.

"We wanted to share the Mauritius we fell in love with—the quiet, the salt, and the slow rhythm of the island."

Our Core Philosophy

bed

Intimate Comfort

With only six curated suites, we ensure an atmosphere of exclusive tranquility. Every fabric is organic linen, every scent is distilled from local blooms, designed to cradle you in barefoot luxury.

restaurant

Island Flavors

Olivier’s kitchen follows the 'Garden-to-Gourmet' ethos. We harvest our own hibiscus, vanilla, and tropical fruits, creating a daily menu that honors Mauritian traditions with a modern, refined edge.

spa

Slow Living

Unplugging is our ultimate amenity. We encourage guests to leave their devices behind and reconnect with the simple joys—a sunrise swim, a shared story, or the rustle of palms.

Our Commitment

Respect for the Lagoon & Island

eco

Zero-Waste Mindset

We operate a plastic-free sanctuary, from our composting kitchen to our refillable botanical amenities.

water_drop

Coral Restoration

A portion of every stay funds our local reef nursery, helping protect the vibrant life of our Mauritius lagoon.

Solar Energy

Harnessing the Mauritian sun, our retreat is powered by 100% renewable energy systems tucked discreetly out of sight.
